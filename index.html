About Me

QA + DEV

Hi, I'm Imo — I build things and break them properly.

I'm a Full-Stack Developer from Uyo, Akwa Ibom, Nigeria, currently transitioning into Quality Assurance engineering. My background is in building scalable backend systems with Node.js, NestJS, and Python — which gives me a unique edge in QA: I understand the code I'm testing, not just the surface.

Outside of code, I teach — I've been an instructor at Aptech and FutureLabs, mentoring students in full-stack development. Teaching forces you to truly understand what you know, and it's made me a better engineer and communicator.
